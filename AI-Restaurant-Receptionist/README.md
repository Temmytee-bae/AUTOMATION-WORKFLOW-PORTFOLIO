Project Overview

The AI-Powered Restaurant Receptionist & Reservation Automation system is an intelligent customer service solution designed to streamline restaurant reservations and guest communication.
Built with n8n and Google Gemini, the workflow processes incoming customer emails, interprets reservation requests, checks booking availability through Google Calendar and supports reservation creation and record management in Google Sheets.
By integrating AI-driven communication with automated booking operations, the solution helps hospitality businesses reduce administrative workload, improve response efficiency and deliver a more organised and responsive guest experience.
